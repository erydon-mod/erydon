## Attribution

When sharing these assets or an adaptation, provide attribution substantially
similar to:

> ERYDON assets by Oliver, licensed under CC BY-SA 4.0.
> Source: https://github.com/erydon-mod/erydon

Also identify material changes and license adaptations under CC BY-SA 4.0 or a
compatible licence, as required by the licence.

## Scope and exceptions

The previous CC BY-SA 4.0 grant covered ERYDON-owned material under:

- `docs/branding/`
- `src/main/resources/assets/erydon/`
- ERYDON-authored overrides under `src/main/resources/assets/minecraft/`
- ERYDON-authored resources under `src/main/resources/data/`
- `src/test/resources/restored-family-geometry/`

It does not cover:

- `src/main/resources/assets/erydon/font/cinzel.ttf`, which is OFL-1.1;
- the Gradle wrapper, which retains the Gradle distribution's licences;
- Minecraft, Fabric, or other external projects and trademarks; or
- separately distributed ERYDON Collection 32x/64x packs unless their own
  distribution explicitly applies this licence.
