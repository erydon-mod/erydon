# Current terms and previous grants

As of 25 October 2026, the current primary terms for Oliver-owned ERYDON code,
assets and documentation are Oliver's Minecraft Mods Restricted License 1.0
in `LICENSE` (`LicenseRef-Oliver-Restricted-1.0`). This is the same primary
licence used across the ERYDON Collection.

## Earlier permissions remain intact

Public ERYDON source through commit
`76b50dda1c5b3768dc35314b3b3b4821a10ceafa` was supplied with MIT terms for code
and CC BY-SA 4.0 terms for Oliver-owned assets. Those grants remain available
for material already supplied under them, including that material when it is
retained in later source or releases. This notice does not withdraw existing
permissions, impose new restrictions on those earlier grants, or change the
terms attached to historical downloads. The restricted licence does not make
previously permissive material exclusively restricted.

The complete earlier MIT grant is retained in `LICENSE-CODE.md`. The earlier
asset scope and attribution are in `LICENSE-ASSETS.md`, with complete legal
code in `LICENSES/CC-BY-SA-4.0.txt`. In a JAR, these files are provided under
`META-INF/licenses/` as `ERYDON-CODE.md`, `ERYDON-ASSETS.md`, and
`CC-BY-SA-4.0.txt`.

## Separate notices remain effective

Explicit MIT notices in helper code and the development toolkit remain in
place. Cinzel remains under OFL-1.1, and the Gradle wrapper keeps its upstream
terms. Material not owned by Oliver is excluded from the restricted licence;
see `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` and the accompanying licence texts. Other
Collection products retain their own notices and previous grants.
